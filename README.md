# Split the check

Split a restaurant bill from a photo of the receipt. Tax, tip and fees are divided by what each person ordered, so nobody pays for someone else's steak.

Open it: https://dirtdiver419.github.io/Split-The-Check/

## How to use it

1. Take a photo of the receipt, or type or paste the items.
2. Add everyone at the table and mark who paid.
3. Tap who had each item. Shared plates can be split evenly or unevenly.
4. Check the tax and pick a tip.
5. Copy or screenshot everyone's share.

## Privacy

Nothing is saved or uploaded. The receipt is read on your own phone, and closing the page erases everything.

---

Not financial advice. Receipt reading can be wrong, so check the items and totals against the receipt before anyone pays.
