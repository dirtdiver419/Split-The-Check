# Split the check

Split a restaurant bill from a photo of the receipt. Tax, tip and fees are split proportionally, by how much each person ordered, so nobody pays for someone else's steak.

Open it: https://dirtdiver419.github.io/Split-The-Check/

## How to use it

1. Take a photo of the receipt, or type or paste the items.
2. Add everyone at the table and mark who paid.
3. Select who had each item. Use **Options** on a row for quantities, add-ons, splitting and more.
4. Check the tax and enter the tip.
5. Copy or screenshot everyone's share.

The full guide, with every button explained, is at https://dirtdiver419.github.io/Split-The-Check/guide.html and linked at the bottom of the site.

## Privacy

Nothing is saved or uploaded. The receipt is read on your own phone, and closing the page erases everything.

---

Not financial advice. Receipt reading can be wrong, so check the items and totals against the receipt before anyone pays.
